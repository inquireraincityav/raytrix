The story

From Mumbai to Vancouver.

Raytrix started spinning in 2005 for private gigs. His ear for a room quickly outgrew the format — over the next decade he built a reputation for reading a crowd with unusual precision, moving from low-key and sophisticated to upbeat and animated in a single set. Along the way he won 12 DJ competitions.

In Mumbai he became a fixture: Club ROYALTY in 2015, then a six-year run as Resident DJ at KITTY SU from 2016 to 2022 — one of India's most legendary nightclubs. In 2019, he was ranked among the Top 100 DJs of India.

He's opened for Peggy Gou, Nastia, Martin Roth, Thomas Gold, EDX, Danny Avila, Tujamo, Dirtcaps and Tavi Castro. And he's played weddings and private parties for Salman Khan, Karan Johar, Anurag Kashyap, Neil Nitin Mukesh, Kunal Khemu, Raj Kundra, Ayesha Jhulka and Karan Wahi.

Today he calls Vancouver home. Within a year of moving to Canada he became the city's most in-demand Bollywood DJ, playing its biggest South-Asian events. When he's not touring, he teaches the next generation as an instructor at the Tabletutors DJ Community.
