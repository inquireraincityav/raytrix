Origin

Born on November 16 in India, Raytrix began spinning in 2005 for private gigs and never really put the headphones down. Over the next decade he built a reputation for reading a room with unusual precision — moving from low-key and sophisticated to upbeat and animated in a single set — and won 12 DJ competitions along the way.

In Mumbai, he became a fixture: Club ROYALTY in 2015, then a six-year residency at KITTY SU from 2016 to 2022 — one of India's most legendary nightclubs. In 2019, he was ranked among the Top 100 DJs of India.

He's opened for Peggy Gou, Nastia, Martin Roth, Thomas Gold, EDX, Danny Avila, Tujamo, Dirtcaps and Tavi Castro. He's played weddings and private parties for Salman Khan, Karan Johar, Anurag Kashyap, Neil Nitin Mukesh, Kunal Khemu, Raj Kundra, Ayesha Jhulka and Karan Wahi.

Today he calls Vancouver home. Within a year of moving to Canada he became the city's most in-demand Bollywood DJ, playing its biggest South-Asian events. When he's not on tour, he teaches the next generation as a DJ instructor at the Tabletutors DJ Community.
