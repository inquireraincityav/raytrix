Behind The Decks.

Raytrix has spent a decade reading rooms and moving them — winning 12 DJ competitions, holding a six-year residency at Mumbai's legendary KITTY SU, and ranking among the Top 100 DJs of India in 2019.

Now based in Vancouver, he's the city's most in-demand Bollywood DJ, plays celebrity weddings across three continents, and mentors the next generation at Tabletutors.
