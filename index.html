The Artist

Sound with altitude.

Sagar Gamre — better known as Raytrix — has spent more than a decade reading rooms and moving them. From resident nights at Mumbai's KITTY SU to headline sets across Europe and the UAE, his style shifts effortlessly between low-key sophistication and full-blown floor energy.

Today he calls Vancouver home, where he's become the city's most in-demand Bollywood DJ and mentors the next generation as a Tabletutors instructor.
