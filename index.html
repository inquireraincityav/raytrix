Meet the artist

Sagar Gamre. Raytrix on stage.

Ten years in and Raytrix still reads a room like a first draft — from low-key and sophisticated to full-blown floor energy in a single set. He's won 12 DJ competitions along the way, held a six-year residency at Mumbai's legendary KITTY SU, and was ranked among the Top 100 DJs of India in 2019.

Today he calls Vancouver home, where he's become the city's most in-demand Bollywood DJ, and mentors the next generation at the Tabletutors DJ Community.
